Link Drive Rd.

Interior design and photorealistic 3D visualization for Link Drive Rd., a bespoke residential project completed for Imperial Homes.

Link Drive Rd. is a 2018 interior design and architectural 3D visualization project completed by RDVS Studios for Imperial Homes.

The project features expansive residential spatial planning, tailored joinery, refined lighting schemes, and meticulous photoreal 3D rendering of master suites, living lounges, and dining environments.

Client Imperial Homes
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2018
Typology Residential
