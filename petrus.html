Petrus

Interior design and photorealistic 3D visualization commissioned by Imperial Homes for Petrus, with architecture by S.A.A.

Petrus is a 2017 interior design and architectural 3D visualization project completed by RDVS Studios for Imperial Homes.

With architecture designed by S.A.A, RDVS developed the comprehensive interior design language, bespoke spatial detailing, material specifications, lighting atmosphere, and high-fidelity photoreal 3D visual simulations.
