Architectural Design & Spatial Design — 2019 · Studio Commission

Funko Ridge Residence

Terraced hillside residential enclave contoured to natural topographic gradients, minimizing site impact and optimizing panoramic ocean views for Funko Ridge Residence.

Funko Ridge Residence forms part of RDVS Studios' enduring catalogue of architectural, interior, and computational spatial explorations. Completed in 2019, the project investigates tactile material boundaries and volumetric precision.

Our multidisciplinary team developed the project from programmatic schematic design through high-fidelity visual simulation and comprehensive turnkey project execution, ensuring strict adherence to the studio's closed-loop methodology.

Client Ridge Estates / Private Client
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2019
Typology Residential
