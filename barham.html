41 Barham Luxury Residence

A minimalist architectural volume embracing high-contrast warm materiality, double-height ceiling voids, and seamless indoor-outdoor courtyards.

Located in the leafy diplomatic precinct of Cantonments, 41 Barham is an essay in residential serenity. The architecture withdraws behind quiet perimeter massing, opening inward toward an expansive private sanctuary featuring reflection pools and shaded tropical verandahs.

The ground level is dominated by an interconnected living and dining pavilion with six-meter double-height voids. Massive sliding glass screens slide into concealed wall cavities, turning the interior living zone into an open-air pavilion cooled by cross-ventilation.

Material selections emphasize permanence and tactile authenticity: bush-hammered local stone, honed basalt pavers, and oiled natural teak millwork executed by RDVS's master craftspeople.

Client Barham Developments
Location Cantonments, Accra
Completion 2025
Gross Floor Area 1,100 sq.m
Scope of Services Architectural Design, Interior Architecture, Lighting Engineering & Turnkey Execution
