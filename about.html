About RDVS

A multidisciplinary practice established on the convergence of digital architectural thinking and direct physical fabrication.

Designing and making things since 2008, Revival Design + VFX Studios (RDVS) is an independent multidisciplinary studio operating from Accra, Ghana. Founded by three creatives with deep architectural backgrounds, our practice undertakes private residential estates, contemporary commercial headquarters, institutional landmarks, and cultural installations across West Africa and internationally.

We are founded on the belief that spatial design cannot be detached from its physical reality. RDVS bridges the gap between speculative 3D conceptualization and tactile, site-built execution. We operate simultaneously as computational visualizers, spatial designers, and general contractors—often collaborating seamlessly with licensed architects when a project's scale and vision demand it.

The Closed-Loop Methodology

This closed-loop methodology ensures that every detail visualized in our digital studio is engineered with acoustic, structural, and material integrity before breaking ground on site.

Our team works collaboratively across technical disciplines, delivering turnkey projects that embody spatial clarity, quiet elegance, and enduring construction craft. Whether we are constructing physical spaces, rendering photorealistic virtual environments, or designing a brand's digital footprint, our work is intentionally designed to move the needle.

What We Do

We offer an end-to-end suite of creative and technical services, allowing us to bridge the gap between digital concepts and physical execution seamlessly.

art.

Bespoke artwork, digital illustration and visual storytelling tailored to elevate ideas, brands, and narratives.

bim.

Architecture, structure and services reconciled in one coordinated model before they reach site, so clash detection, quantities and documentation share a single source of truth.

competitions.

Pushing the boundaries of conceptual design and architectural thought leadership — competition entries, and the pitches that test a scheme before a client commits to it.

design.

One method across eight disciplines — architectural, interior, industrial & furniture, graphic, illustration, art direction, motion, and digital & web design. Geometry, light and material studied first, drawn second, documented precisely enough to build from.

design + build.

A seamless, turnkey approach—handling every phase of a project from creative conception to final physical construction.

in-house.

Self-initiated work. Identity studies and speculative schemes where the brief, the budget and the deadline are all ours.

photography.

Built work photographed after completion — interiors, shops and office floors — and the location plates shot for environmental graphics and print mockups. Framing, light and material given the same attention as a render.

vfx + cgi.

Encompassing 3D visualization, 3D animation, and architectural visualization (arch viz). High-fidelity photorealistic rendering, cinematic environment simulation, and digital artistry that bring unbuilt spaces and complex spatial visions to life before breaking ground.
