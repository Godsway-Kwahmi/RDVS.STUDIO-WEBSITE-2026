About

A multidisciplinary practice established on the convergence of digital architectural thinking and direct physical fabrication.

RDVS is an independent multidisciplinary studio operating across architecture, interior design, visual effects (VFX), motion design, graphic systems, industrial design, and turnkey construction.

Founded on the belief that spatial design cannot be detached from its physical reality, RDVS bridges the gap between speculative 3D conceptualization and tactile, site-built execution. We operate simultaneously as spatial architects, computational visualizers, and general contractors.

This closed-loop methodology ensures that every detail visualized in our digital studio is engineered with acoustic, structural, and material integrity before breaking ground on site. Our team works collaboratively across technical disciplines, delivering turnkey projects that embody spatial clarity, quiet elegance, and enduring construction craft.

Headquartered in Accra, Ghana, our practice undertakes private residential estates, contemporary commercial headquarters, institutional landmarks, and cultural installations across West Africa and internationally.
