Motion Design / 3D Animation — 2020 · RDVS Studios

Caustics RnD

One effect holds the screen: the rippled webs of light that moving water throws across the floor and the edge of a pool, with the shadows of palm fronds falling behind it.

The work signs itself “CAUSTICS_001 / an RDVS study. / September 2020”, and that signature is the whole of its paperwork: an in-house study made by RDVS Studios and dated September 2020 by its own title card. No client commissioned it, no location is named on it, and no individual designer is credited anywhere in the file.

It was rendered in Corona Renderer 5 and cut as a single 25.7-second film at 1920×1080, which is everything the study left behind. The listing carries that film alone. The one still published beside it is a frame lifted from the film at its own 1920×1080, used as the player poster and as the homepage slide before the script runs, so nothing published here is a lesser copy of a shot you can already watch.
