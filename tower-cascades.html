Residential / Interior Design & Architectural Visualization — 2017 · Hawkrad Properties

Tower Cascades

Interior design and CGI for Hawkrad Properties: twenty-four visualisation plates, an animation film and a VR walkthrough covering the Tower Cascades apartments and their shared amenities — the lobby, reception, roof-terrace lounge and fitness floor — made to be used as marketing material for the development.

The studio took the interior scheme and the imagery; the architecture is ArchXenus’s. The set moves through an apartment — principal bedroom, open dining and living volume, media lounge, kitchen, bathroom and walk-in wardrobes — and then through the building’s shared spaces, where a black feature wall carries large Welcome lettering behind the reception desk.

The frames were rendered between October and December 2017, with a later round of lighting passes in February 2018, and the gallery went up on Behance on 3 April 2018 as “TOWER CASCADES - INTERIOR DESIGN + 3D VISUALIZATION”. Every plate below is from that gallery: Tower Cascades on Behance.
