3D Visualization — 2016 · Commissioned Work

Weldment Panels

Renders of a graffiti-proof wall panel system, made in 2016 as part of a client’s presentation material.

The panels are the subject, not the buildings around them. Each view places the same cladding on a piece of urban concrete — a curved road overpass, a retaining wall beside a carriageway, a rail cutting, a bridge pier and the undercroft of a deck — so the product could be judged in the settings it was being sold into.

The facing itself reads as a dark perforated or mesh sheet carrying a scatter of small colour chips, dense enough to break up any mark and to keep a tagged wall from reading as a tag. Night plates push that effect hardest: with ground uplighters raking across the surface the panel glitters, and the colour that survives is the panel’s own rather than anything sprayed on it.

Context is deliberately generic — a stock high-rise skyline, ordinary traffic, plain concrete — so nothing competes with the cladding. Daylight plates show the run of the wall and how it terminates at a corner or a parapet; the dusk and night plates show how it behaves once the structure it sits inside is lit.

Eight views were published as WELDMENT PANELS — 3D VISUALIZATION; the set is on Behance.
