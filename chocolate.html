Identity / Graphic Design — 2014 · Private Client

Chocolate

Logo design for a fashion design house in Ghana.

Chocolate is the identity RDVS Studios created for Chocolate by Kwaku Bediako, a Ghanaian fashion design house. The mark draws the letter C from a couturier's dress form, finished with a drip that reads as melted chocolate and as the tail of a garment left on the stand.

The system covers a primary logotype pairing the mark with a script wordmark, a winged badge variant for smaller applications, a companion corporate typeface, and stationery. The badge was produced as metal hardware and applied to the house's footwear and leatherwear.

Documentation was published to our Behance archive in December 2014. View the original project on Behance. Images courtesy Chocolate by Kwaku Bediako.
