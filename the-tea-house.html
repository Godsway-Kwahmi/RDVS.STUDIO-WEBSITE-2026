The Tea House

An identity system and bespoke UI/UX web platform for The Tea House, a tea room and event venue in Accra: a leaf mark in two greens, ochre display type and a set of organic overlapping shapes carried across the site's story, menu, booking and reservation flows.

The Tea House was delivered in 2021 for a private client. The brand is built on a two-tone leaf mark and an ochre-and-olive palette, with large ghosted section titles and overlapping organic shapes used as both decoration and cropping device. Those shapes carry through the site’s circular image masks, so the photography of the kitchen, the diners and the garden all sit inside the same silhouette as the logo.

The eighteen plates below are the Adobe XD artboards for the website at 1920 × 1080: the homepage and brand story, the location, help and policy screens, the menu with its dish detail modal, three priced booking packages — Baby at GHC 200, Business at GHC 300 and Titan at GHC 700 — the spaces and events pages, and the reservation panel. It is a graphic design and digital & web design commission — the identity, the art direction and the site’s screens — and it was published on Behance on 24 August 2021.
