Expertise

A multidisciplinary practice spanning spatial architecture, digital visual effects, brand systems, and physical fabrication. We dissolve traditional boundaries between digital ideation and physical construction.

Art

Experimental computational art, digital illustration, and bespoke visual compositions tailored to elevate ideas, brands, and narratives. We explore algorithmic aesthetics, abstract form studies, and high-resolution generative visuals at the intersection of fine art and spatial design.

BIM

Coordinated building information models. Architecture, structure and services reconciled in a single model before they reach site, so clash detection, quantity take-offs and construction documentation come from one source of truth rather than being redrawn discipline by discipline.

Competitions

Pushing the boundaries of conceptual design and speculative architecture on the global stage. We develop provocative, research-driven competition entries and masterplan propositions that challenge conventions of typology, spatial density, and environmental performance. The same exploratory method drives the pitches and proposals that test a scheme before a client commits to it.

Design

The studio’s drawing-board practice: seven disciplines sharing one method. Geometry, light and material are studied first, drawn second, and handed over as documentation precise enough to build from.

Architectural Design

Contextual architectural design anchored in geometry, climate responsiveness, and structural clarity. Our work spans private residential sanctuaries, multi-tiered mixed-use developments, corporate office towers, and civic institutions. Services encompass masterplanning, volumetric zoning, facade engineering, and full municipal working documentation.

Art Direction

Concept and visual direction for work that has to hold together across formats. We set the idea and the rules that carry it — type, palette, imagery, tone — then direct the shoots, renders and layouts so an identity reads as one argument.

Digital & Web Design

Minimalist, performance-first digital web design and interactive experiences. We design responsive web architecture, portfolio platforms, and bespoke web interfaces focused on pristine typography, negative space, fast load speeds, and intuitive digital interactions without unnecessary ornament.

Graphic Design

Understated, typographic-led graphic design and visual identity systems. We develop comprehensive brand identities, editorial monographs, spatial signage, environmental wayfinding, corporate stationery, and packaging design rooted in minimalist European and Swiss modernist principles.

Industrial & Furniture Design

Product, furniture, and tactile object design crafted with ergonomic precision and raw material honesty. We design bespoke monolithic conference tables, ergonomic executive furnishings, custom architectural lighting fixtures, acoustic ceiling baffles, and modular joinery fabricated to rigorous tolerances.

Interior Design

Complete interior curation, material palette selection, and bespoke FF&E (furniture, fixtures, and equipment) procurement. We curate tactile textiles, custom upholstery, natural stone accents, decorative lighting, and curated art arrangements that evoke calm sophistication and timeless modernist warmth across private estates and executive suites.

Motion Design

Temporal visual storytelling through dynamic typographic choreography, animated spatial walkthroughs, and abstract broadcast packaging. We translate brand identity into motion for digital displays, architectural video walls, interactive presentations, and cinematic commercial spots.

Direct physical translation of digital architecture. As general contractors with integrated carpentry and fabrication shops, we oversee on-site trade coordination, custom timber milling, metalwork fabrication, and structural execution. We ensure what was visualized in 3D is realized identically in the physical world.

VFX + CGI

High-end cinematic visual effects, digital environment synthesis, and spatial simulation. Our VFX + CGI practice encompasses photorealistic 3D visualization, architectural visualization (arch viz), 3D animation, and advanced digital environment rendering. Utilizing physically based rendering (PBR) and optical ray tracing, we simulate daylight dynamics, atmospheric depth, and tactile material physics to bring unbuilt spaces and complex spatial concepts to life.
