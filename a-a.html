Residential / Interior Remodeling — 2023 · Volta Region, Ghana

A-A

Master bathroom and walk-in closet remodeling balancing refined materiality, louvre fenestration, and serene natural lighting.

A-A is a master bathroom and walk-in closet renovation located in the Volta Region, Ghana. Originally modeled in 2022/2023 and re-rendered in 2025, the project showcases tactile material boundaries and volumetric precision.

Interior architecture and 3D visualization by RDVS Studios, exploring seamless transitions between bespoke joinery, open glass shower volumes, and daylight filtration through tropical timber louvres.

Client Private Client
