Macord International School

A complete 2024 commission spanning facade design, landscape design, interior design, industrial & furniture design and photoreal 3D visualization for Macord International School.

Macord International School presents a new street-facing facade articulated in rhythmic coloured fins beneath a planted terrace edge, fronted by a landscaped arrival forecourt and perimeter landscape works. Interiors and a bespoke industrial & furniture package complete the commission across the school's learning and administration spaces.

Our multidisciplinary team developed the project from facade concept and landscape strategy through interior detailing and furniture design to high-fidelity 3D visualization, simulating every published view before delivery. The project was designed by Jude Nyoagbe.

Client Macord International School
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2024
Typology Educational
Scope of Services Facade Design (Architecture), Landscape Design, Interior Design, Industrial & Furniture Design & 3D Visualization
Team Jude Nyoagbe
