Digital Youth Village (DYV). Exterior at dawn. Greater Accra, Ghana.

Architecture by @_prediosgroup

Renders by @rdvs.studio

The film above is a fifteen-second monochrome push-in through the central atrium: a dense cluster of glowing cylindrical pendants under the domed opening, curved balcony bands rising around it, and lounge groups set among indoor trees. It opens on the same atrium view the gallery holds as its fourth plate, and it is the studio’s own capture posted to Instagram, so it is 1068×720 rather than a master render.
