Space / Corporate Build — 2024 · Accra Financial Enclave

MTN Headquarters Executive Wing

Continuous rhythm of warm timber fins and diffused recessed light guides circulation through executive conference suites.

For telecommunications giant MTN Group, RDVS transformed an entire executive floorplate into an acoustically isolated, rhythmically unified corporate sanctuary. The central corridor functions as both an arterial passage and an experiential buffer between high-density operational zones and leadership boardrooms.

The primary design intervention features vertical solid timber fins calibrated to absorb high-frequency speech frequencies while concealing service panels, HVAC distribution, and biometric security portals.

Delivered under our turnkey build division, all timber elements were pre-milled offsite and installed with zero visible fasteners, achieving impeccable architectural continuity across the executive wing.

Client MTN Group
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2024
Gross Floor Area 950 sq.m
Scope of Services Spatial Architecture, Acoustic Engineering, Custom Millwork & General Construction
