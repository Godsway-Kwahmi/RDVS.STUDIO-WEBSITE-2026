Product / Modern Indigenous Goods — 2021 · Accra, Ghana

MIG

Modern Indigenous Goods studio identity and spatial concept honoring authentic African material heritage.

MIG (Modern Indigenous Goods) represents a curated design narrative synthesizing indigenous West African fabrication methods with sharp contemporary aesthetics.

Digital direction and visualization by RDVS Studios, exploring object taxonomy, earth pigments, and minimal spatial displays.

Client MIG Brand
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2021
Typology Studio Concept & Product Identity
Services Graphic Design·Industrial & Furniture Design
Team RDVS Team

Film

The four-and-a-half-second flip: the four marks of the MIG monogram start edge-on as one flat band, then rotate up into the picture plane one after another from the left, their extruded sides catching grey light as they turn, and resolve into the flat black logotype on white the page opens with, which holds to the end
