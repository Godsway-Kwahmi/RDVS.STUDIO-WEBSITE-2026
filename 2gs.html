Identity / Graphic Design — 2015 · 2GS Construction + Logistics

2GS

A 2015 identity for 2GS Construction + Logistics: a monogram built from the idea of the collective, carried from pencil construction studies through stationery, business cards, letterheads and concrete signage.

The brief asked for an identity that embodies the essential ethos of a construction company in every facet — dynamism, accuracy, efficiency, firmness, refinement and elegance — beginning with the logo and identity design, which would then inform the letterheads and the rest of the company stationery. The logo was to act as the ideal PR proxy, and a deliberate deviation from construction clichés was to shape the first interactions between client and brand.

The concept is the collective: to construct is to assemble, so that the resulting synergies work together to produce the most apt of products, and to provide logistics is to provide the apt aide and aid to serve a purpose well. Simple geometric shapes reinforce that idea of a collective and its result, while the accompanying motif — a labyrinth — stands metaphorically for the meticulous, complex care a proficient construction pays to detail and accuracy.

The palette and textures are drawn from the materials of the business itself: stone, sand and concrete, wood, steel and iron, resolved into five swatches of stone grey, pale gold, copper, taupe and grey, with the system set in Titillium. Bold but iconic elements let a customer interface with the brand at a glance, with no misunderstanding.

The plates follow the identity from first principles to finished artefacts: the monogram pencilled in construction lines on gridded paper, six wireframe studies on a construction grid, the lockup matrix in stacked and horizontal arrangements, business cards in white and black variants patterned with the maze motif and a sand chevron, letterheads over a faint maze ground, the full stationery suite with its stamp, and finally the monogram cast as dimensional concrete signage on a slate wall.

The gallery was published to Behance in August 2015 as 2GS BRANDING — GRAPHIC DESIGN, credited there to Randy Biney and Jude Nyoagbe for concept and Randy Biney for design; the full set is on Behance.
