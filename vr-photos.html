Spatial Photography & CGI / 360° Panorama — 2024 · Studio Commission

VR Photos

Interactive 360-degree spatial photography and high-fidelity ray-traced spherical captures presenting seamless panoramic spatial immersion.

VR Photos brings high-resolution spatial architecture into full spherical fidelity. Each capture is an equirectangular projection balancing light transmission, reflections, material textures, and spatial depth.

Click and drag with your mouse, swipe on touchscreens, or use the directional look-around controls to rotate through the environment. Explore architectural volumes with total visual freedom.
