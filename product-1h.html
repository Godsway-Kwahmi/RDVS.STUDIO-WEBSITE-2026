MIG / Furniture / Media wall — 2016

1H

A wall run built from modules rather than one fixed elevation: open bays and closed volumes re-stack around a screen at eye level. RDVS designed 1H in 2016 for the MIG line and documented it in two schemes, a dark-stained set stacked as a tall bay and a white-and-dark set run wider across the wall.

Six plates cover two arrangements. The first is a tall bay in a dark-stained finish, the screen in its central open bay with a media player on the shelf above and books below; the second runs wider and paler, white shelf plates and dark door modules framing the screen, a lit niche carrying two pale wooden vases.

Open and closed storage alternate through both arrangements, and the screen stays at eye level in each. Two of the plates are the same corner of the dark set, shot straight and then canted so the grain across the door fronts reads as one field.

The lighting is part of the set rather than the room. Each arrangement is floated on black, and the night plate of the white scheme is the same build with the room down and the niche left lit.

1H is industrial design and product visualization: RDVS designed the wall for the studio’s own MIG line and rendered it as a product. The code fits the naming the rest of the line carries, HG-DESK, SP001, S-AGE and MOUND among them.

2016 is the date the studio records for 1H. The media files carry a later export stamp, which is not the design date.
