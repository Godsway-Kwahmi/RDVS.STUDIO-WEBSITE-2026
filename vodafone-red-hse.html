Architecture / Architectural Design — 2011 · Vodafone

Vodafone Red Hse

An initial design proposal for a Vodafone Red House, drawn in July 2011 with Steven Ntow — the brand lockup and the building’s corner set side by side on one sheet.

The sheet is half identity and half building. At the top left the vodafone wordmark is reversed out of a red block with the speech mark beside it, and below that Red House is set as two stacked words in heavy red capitals over a hairline rule. A single line declares the work an initial design proposal, with the middle word dropped to a ghost weight so the eye reads INITIAL and PROPOSAL first; underneath, the credit names the authors — Steven Ntow and R.D+V.S — and dates the drawing July 2011.

The rest of the sheet is a corner view of the building, drawn flat and monochrome rather than rendered photorealistically: pale grey sky, black line-work, and a facade of horizontal louvers wrapping the corner of the upper volume. The speech mark is applied twice, cut as a large outline into the slatted wall and picked out again on the dark fascia band at the top right, where Red House is lettered in outline capitals so that it reads against the slats. Below the louvered band the ground storey is fully glazed, with a second, taller row of slats returning along the side elevation.

Nothing on the sheet says the scheme was built, and it was never published to our Behance archive, so this proposal is the whole record of the project.

Client Vodafone
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2011
Typology Architecture
Services Architectural Design
Scope of Services Initial design proposal: corner massing and louvered facade, signage and brand application, and the Red House wordmark lockup.
