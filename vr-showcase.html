Space / Commercial Build — 2010 · Accra, Ghana

VR SHOWCASE

A sculptured corporate reception and executive suite featuring bespoke faceted acoustics, continuous glass partitioning, and turnkey timber fabrication.

Commissioned to redefine the corporate arrival experience for VR SHOWCASE Corporation in Accra, RDVS envisioned a spatial threshold that reconciles high-contrast architectural geometry with tactile material warmth. The design removes visual clutter, guiding visitors through an intuitive sequence of transitional zones.

The primary architectural gesture consists of faceted geometric walling integrated with concealed acoustic damping, preventing reverberation within the double-height circulation areas. Floor-to-ceiling acoustic glass partitions establish visual transparency across executive meeting suites while preserving strict speech privacy.

As a turnkey design + build commission, every detail was drafted in our digital studio and fabricated directly by our specialized millwork team. Custom timber joinery, flush architectural doors, and integrated lighting channels ensure flawless alignment from digital concept to physical reality.

Client VR SHOWCASE Corporation
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2010
Gross Floor Area 1,450 sq.m
Scope of Services Interior Architecture, Spatial Branding, Industrial Design, Custom Millwork & Turnkey Construction
