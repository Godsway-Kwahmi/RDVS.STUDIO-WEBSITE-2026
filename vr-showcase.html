Visual Effects (VFX) & CGI / VR Simulation — 2024 · Studio Commission

VR Showcase

00:00 / 00:00

Immersive 360-degree real-time spatial simulation exploring photorealistic lighting, dynamic material boundary transitions, and continuous 360° interactive visual navigation.

VR Showcase pushes the limits of computational visual architecture. Created by the RDVS Virtual Reality Studio, the project delivers a fully interactive spherical walkthrough where users can navigate 360 degrees through tailored spatial volumes, observing ray-traced ambient illumination, tactile concrete surfaces, and custom executive interiors.

Use the on-screen look-around controls, drag with your mouse, or swipe on mobile to explore every angle in full 360-degree freedom. The simulation represents our multidisciplinary approach uniting spatial design with real-time immersive computing.

Client RDVS Virtual Lab
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2024
Typology VR Simulation
Scope of Services 360° VR Simulation, Visual Effects (VFX) & CGI, Interactive 3D
Team Godsway Kwahmi, RDVS Virtual Reality Team
