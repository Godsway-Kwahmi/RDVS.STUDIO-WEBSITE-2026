Residential / Interior Design & Architectural Visualization — 2012 · Devtraco

C25

C25 is a single-storey family house that RDVS visualized for Devtraco in 2012, pairing interior design with architectural visualization to present the lounge, bedroom and street elevations as a coherent set.

Devtraco commissioned RDVS to help buyers picture a compact modern house before it was built. The brief asked for a warm, lived-in interior and clean exterior elevations that read as one project rather than separate rooms and facades. Working from the architect’s layout, the studio modeled the whole property so that the public spaces, the bedroom and the street approach could all be shown from believable camera angles.

The plan opens onto a single L-shaped lounge where a cream sectional sofa wraps a low black coffee table, keeping circulation clear toward the dining area. A recessed cove-lit ceiling and a mustard feature wall anchor the seating, while the bedroom sits apart as a quieter, wood-lined room. Outside, the house spreads as a low horizontal volume with a paved forecourt sized for parking and arrival.

Inside, the palette stays neutral and warm: off-white sofas, dark timber furniture and a single ochre wall printed with pale floral motifs. Concealed cove lighting washes the ceiling and keeps the mood soft, with a bright shaft of daylight across the bedroom floor. Outside, white render is lifted by accent panels in brick, green and red, and by timber soffits that glow under evening light.

The finished set runs seven plates, moving from the lounge and bedroom interiors through four exterior views of the street approach. Every image carries a small studio logo and a New Life 2.0 series stamp in the corner, tying the frames together as one presentation. Released online in 2012, the gallery sold the house to buyers as a finished, ready-to-move-in product.

The project was published as C25 — DEVTRACO — RDVS. DESIGN; the full set is on Behance.
