Space / Interior Architecture — 2024 · Coastal Enclave

La Beach Towers Penthouse

Panoramic coastal luxury interior framing expansive oceanic vistas through minimalist double-height glazing and bespoke low-slung joinery.

Overlooking the Gulf of Guinea, this penthouse interior was designed as a tranquil contemplation space suspended between sky and sea. The layout deliberately lowers furniture datums to ensure horizon lines remain completely unobstructed from any vantage point in the open-plan gallery.

A restricted material palette of hand-finished microcement, fluted limestone wall paneling, and brushed smoked oak creates tactile intimacy while softening harsh midday coastal glare. Recessed perimeter coves introduce warm indirect illumination as evening approaches.

Every element of the living and dining furniture was custom-developed by RDVS's industrial design practice, pairing ergonomic precision with enduring minimalist aesthetics.

Client Private Residence
Location Coastal Enclave, Accra
Completion 2024
Gross Floor Area 480 sq.m
Scope of Services Luxury Interior Architecture, Industrial Furniture Design, Lighting Engineering & High-End 3D Simulation
