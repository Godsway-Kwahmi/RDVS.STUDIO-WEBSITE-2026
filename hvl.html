Brochure / Graphic Design — 2011 · Hili View Lodge

HVL

A two-spread brochure designed in 2011 for Hili View Lodge, a hillside guest lodge at Aburi, laying out the cover, the welcome page and the services, facilities, rooms and location columns as graphic design by RDVS Studio.

The task was a print identity for a small lodge that had the rooms but not yet the voice. Hili View Lodge sits at Aburi in the Eastern Region with the capital visible in the distance, and the brochure had to explain the offer in a glance: a quiet getaway with serviced rooms, a pool and secure parking.

The layout runs on a deep maroon ground with a faint damask texture, orange and cream type, and a heavy display face stacked into short words: Cozy, Breathtaking, Get-away on the cover and Homely, Vivacious, Luxurious inside. Photographs sit in hard-edged blocks, and the tagline More Than A Hint Of Serenity is set beside the HVL monogram.

The inner spread carries a welcome paragraph, a lounge photograph and three insets for the kitchen, a bedroom and the corridor, with four narrow columns headed Services, Facilities, Rooms and Location setting out the offer. Delivered as two print spreads for one folded brochure, the piece gave the lodge a single sheet to hand to enquiries.

The project was published as HVL BROCHURE — RDVS. DESIGN; the full set is on Behance.

Client Hili View Lodge
Location Aburi, Ghana
Completion 2011
Typology Brochure
Services Graphic Design
