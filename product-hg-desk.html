MIG / Furniture / Desk — 2020

HG-DESK

A work desk built around one thick top with a returned edge carrying the MIG mark, set on angled trestle supports. Designed by RDVS in 2020 for the MIG line.

HG-DESK is furniture design, product visualization and in-house work at once: RDVS designed the desk for the studio’s own MIG line and presented it as a product visualization.

The top is the piece: a single heavy hardwood slab, stained black, with its edge returned downward so the MIG mark prints on the vertical face rather than on the writing surface. The base is a pair of angled steel trestles that carry the top clear of the floor at both ends.

Three plates document it: the desk in the studio, a close view of the corner where the stained edge turns, a macro of the grain along the top edge.

2020 is the date the studio records for HG-DESK. The image files carry a later export stamp, which is not the design date.
