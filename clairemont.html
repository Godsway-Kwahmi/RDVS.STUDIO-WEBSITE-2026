Brochure / Architectural Visualization & Graphic Design — 2011 · Private Client

Clairemont

A seventeen-plate brochure RDVS. Design created in 2011 for an architecture firm marketing Clairemont, a contemporary apartment development in Accra, blending graphic design, wayfinding maps and furnished floor plans with architectural visualization renders.

Clairemont is a sales brochure RDVS. Design produced in 2011 for an architecture firm promoting a new apartment development in Accra. The brief called for a printed document that could present an unbuilt project persuasively: explaining where it sits, what it offers and how the unit types work, long before any resident moved in.

The set is organized like a small publication: cover, contents, an introduction spread, an orange wayfinding map of the site among Accra landmarks, feature and amenity pages, then interior and exterior visualization spreads. A second half carries furnished floor plans for the Xara, Maisha, Penthouse and Triplex modules, closing with contact and back cover pages.

Graphically the brochure runs on a single saturated orange against white, cut by diagonal bands and softened with halftone dot gradients. The same stripe system ties photo-style renders, flat maps and technical plans into one voice, while bold sans-serif headings for CONTENT, FEATURES, INTERIORS and EXTERIORS give the reader fast landmarks when flipping through.

The finished document combines RDVS. Design’s own architectural visualization with its graphic layout, giving the firm a ready-to-print marketing tool for pitching Clairemont to prospective buyers. Published plate by plate, the gallery shows how cover, map, feature pages, plan set and back matter hold together as one continuous printed piece.

The project was published as CLAIREMONT APARTMENTS BROCHURE — RDVS. DESIGN; the full set is on Behance.
