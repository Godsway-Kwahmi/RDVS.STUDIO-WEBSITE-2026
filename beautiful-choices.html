Beautiful Choices

Rendering and animation for a poster series designed by Dela Anyaa.

Beautiful Choices presents a poster series designed by Dela Anyaa as a physical set of printed sheets. Every panel carries the same composition — a solid colour field, a high-contrast portrait, a column of body copy and the designer's signature — printed across five colourways: yellow, silver, cyan, magenta and green.

RDVS Studios modelled the sheets and staged them in a seamless white set, fanning them out from a single standing panel so the sequence reads as one design resolving into its variants. The still holds the series at its widest spread; the animation carries the camera through the same arrangement.

Modelling, animation and rendering were led by Jude Nyoagbe, with post-processing and compositing by Randy Biney and sound effects sourced from SoundJay. Documentation was published to our Behance archive in September 2014. View the original project on Behance. Poster design and artwork courtesy Dela Anyaa.
