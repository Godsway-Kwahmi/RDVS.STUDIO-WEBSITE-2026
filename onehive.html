OneHive Innovation Center

A high-concept technology incubator pairing organic fluid contours with integrated digital display matrices and acoustic ceiling baffles.

Commissioned by venture incubator OneHive, this project investigated computational form-generation applied to collaborative workspaces. The architecture rejects rigid rectilinear cubicles in favor of organic spatial pods configured around centralized communal hubs.

Our VFX and visualization team developed custom algorithms to optimize ceiling baffle curvature, ensuring acoustic absorption matches specific speech zones. Lighting elements were choreographed to mirror circadian cycles, transitioning from energizing daylight spectrums to warm evening ambience.

The resulting visual and motion identity package provided OneHive with a comprehensive architectural and brand language deployed across digital media, investor pitches, and on-site physical prototyping.

Client OneHive Venture Studio
Location Accra Tech Park
Completion 2025
Gross Floor Area 1,800 sq.m
Scope of Services Parametric Modeling, Lighting Simulation, VFX Motion Direction & Brand Identity
