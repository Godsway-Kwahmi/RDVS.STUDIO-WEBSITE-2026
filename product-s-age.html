MIG / Furniture / Reception desk — 2018

S-AGE

A curved front-of-house desk in white, its plain panel carrying an information-desk pictogram. Designed by RDVS in 2018 for the MIG line.

S-AGE is furniture design, product visualization and in-house work at once: RDVS designed the desk for the studio’s own MIG line and presented it as a product visualization.

The form is a curved desk with a plain front panel carrying the information-desk pictogram, so the piece states its own function rather than needing signage above it. It is worked in plywood and hardwood and finished white, with a low return at one end deep enough to hold a vase.

Five plates: two product views of the desk with two screens and a vase of greenery on the top, the desk installed in a lobby and seen past a plant, and two details — the pictogram printed on the panel, and the glass vase of pale green flowers standing in the low return.

2018 is the date the studio records for S-AGE. The image files carry a later export stamp, which is not the design date.
