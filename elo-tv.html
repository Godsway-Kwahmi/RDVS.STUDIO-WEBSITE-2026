Graphic Design / Motion Design — 2015 · Studio Commission

ELO TV

A 2015 brand identity and broadcast motion package for ELO TV — logotype, stationery system and a kinetic on-air ident built around the channel's slanted wordmark.

The identity centres on a forward-slanting ELO wordmark carrying a halftone velocity trail and a red parallelogram “tv” flag, tuned to read at broadcast scale and in print alike. The system extends across stationery, business cards and promotional surfaces, with a pixel-shatter motif carrying the brand's digital edge.

For broadcast the mark becomes a kinetic ident: the letterforms open as apertures over live-action footage of presenters and performers, resolving frame by frame into the locked logo. The package was delivered as graphic design and motion design for the channel's 2015 launch.

Client ELO TV
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2015
Typology Broadcast
