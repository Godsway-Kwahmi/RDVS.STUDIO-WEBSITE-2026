Architecture / Civic Build — 2023 · Institutional Enclave

PURC Institutional Complex

Monolithic civic architecture combining deep louvered facades, robust masonry massing, and monumental public entry porticos.

For the Public Utilities Regulatory Commission, RDVS authored an institutional campus that communicates governmental stability and public transparency. The architectural expression utilizes crisp repetitive colonnades that shield administrative offices from the severe afternoon sun.

A broad civic plaza leads to an elevated entry portal framing a central landscaped atrium. This central court acts as a natural thermodynamic chimney, drawing warm air up and away from the building interior while maintaining shaded microclimates for staff and visitors.

Our construction management arm supervised on-site civil works, structural reinforced concrete pouring, and high-tolerance facade stonework through to final governmental commissioning.

Client Public Utilities Regulatory Commission
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2023
Gross Floor Area 3,200 sq.m
Scope of Services Civic Architecture, Structural Coordination & General Construction Management
