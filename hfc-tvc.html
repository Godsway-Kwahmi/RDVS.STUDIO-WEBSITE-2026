Visual Effects (VFX) & Motion Design — 2016 · Commercial Commission

HFC TVC

Visual effects, 3D animation, and motion design for HFC TVC, a broadcast commercial campaign created for production company Midnight Run.

HFC TVC is a 2016 visual effects and motion design project produced by RDVS Studios in collaboration with production company Midnight Run.

The project encompasses intricate 3D kinetic animation, simulation, dynamic Rube Goldberg mechanical visual metaphors, digital compositing, and broadcast-ready motion direction for television.

Client Midnight Run
Production Company Midnight Run
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2016
Typology Commercial / TVC
Services Motion Design·3D Animation
Visual Effects & Motion Design RDVS Studios
Team Jude Abbey, Jude Nyoagbe, Randy Biney
