Brownie’s Place

A 2017 commission from the Accra developer Six Acres: RDVS Studios redesigned the facade of Brownie’s Place, furnished and lit its interiors, rendered the whole set in 3D and packaged the imagery into a marketing brochure.

Six Acres, the Accra real estate developer behind Brownie’s Place, brought RDVS Studios in with a house that needed both a clearer public face and a credible interior story. The brief ran in two parts: redesign the facade and dress the principal rooms, then turn whatever the renders produced into sales material a buyer could hold.

The interiors are planned around a single open living and dining volume, with the dining table held between two tall windows that look onto clipped evergreen hedges. From there the set moves through two bedrooms, a U-shaped kitchen with a worktop island, and a bathroom where a dark tiled shower niche anchors an otherwise pale room.

Palette and finish do most of the work: white gloss cabinetry, pale marble-effect tiles and grey plank floors set against warm oak, charcoal textiles and one deliberate red accent, first as a recessed wall behind the television, then as a large abstract canvas and again in the dining chair cushions. Evening versions of each view layer warm downlights against blue dusk.

Outside, the redesigned facade follows the same logic: white rendered volumes, flat rooflines and metal balustrades warmed by a timber screen, shown at midday and again at dusk. Thirty-three plates carry the interior and facade set; the final six document the brochure built from them, from cover and contents pages to a location map spread.

The project was published as Brownie&#039;s Place — Facade Design + 3D visualization — RDVS. DESIGN; the full set is on Behance and the companion set.
