Product / Architectural Visualization — 2022 · Furniture design by AFK

Ackon Desk

Sculptural solid timber executive desk balancing robust organic joinery with clean architectural silhouettes.

Designed and fabricated by AFK, the Ackon Desk represents a rigorous exploration of mass, shadow lines, and West African hardwood craft.

3D visualization and digital representation by RDVS Studios, documenting the tactile surface treatment, precision mitres, and ergonomic proportions of the desk.

Client AFK
Location Accra, Ghana
Completion 2022
Typology Furniture & Product Design
Services Architectural Visualization
