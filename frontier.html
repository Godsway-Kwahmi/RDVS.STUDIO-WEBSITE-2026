Frontier Commercial Complex

A striking vertical facade composition optimizing solar shading and environmental efficiency for high-density metropolitan commerce.

Situated within the rapidly evolving Airport City business district, Frontier Tower investigates how energy efficiency can dictate architectural expression. The building envelope utilizes an external lattice of high-performance composite shading fins calibrated to solar azimuth angles.

This passive climate-responsive envelope reduces interior thermal load by over 38%, while allowing floor-to-ceiling vision glass to flood commercial floor plates with glare-free ambient natural light.

RDVS carried out comprehensive 3D daylight rendering and computational wind studies to validate microclimate performance before completing technical construction drawings.

Client Frontier Properties
