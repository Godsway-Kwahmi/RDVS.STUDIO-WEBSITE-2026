Digital / Digital & Web Design — 2023 · RDVS Studios

RDVS Studio Digital Platform

The studio’s own site, built around a macro shot of a lens aperture and one line of intent: “Welcome, we are building a new home.”

A pale-blue nav strip sits across the very top — About Us, View Portfolio, Request Callback, Free Consultation, Join the Team, Contact Us — over the full-bleed aperture render. The wordmark at left still spells the practice out in full as Revival Design + VFX Studios, with the initialism carried by the mark.

The headline is set as a condensed grotesque at display size and left-aligned, with a single supporting line beneath it. Below the fold a “clients we’ve proudly worked with” row runs the logos of Imperial, Infinite, Nest and Hubtel, and the page closes on a strip of social icons.

The plate shown here is a capture of the landing screen rather than a design sheet, so it documents the page as it went out rather than the grid it was drawn on.
