Residential / Interior & Architecture — 2023 · East Legon Hills, Accra

TTD

Expansive residential interior suite for D - TWO, encompassing living, dining, and custom culinary spaces.

TTD is a comprehensive residential interior architecture commission located in East Legon Hills, Accra. Developed for the D - TWO residence, the project articulates multi-layered spatial flow across forty detailed plates.

Design and visualization executed in full by RDVS Studios. The interiors balance double-height entertaining halls, bespoke quartz kitchen islands, ribbed feature timber walls, and seamless indoor-outdoor sightlines.

Client Private Client
Location East Legon Hills, Accra, Ghana
