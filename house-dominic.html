Residential / Architectural Design & Architectural Visualization — 2009 · Adolph Amevor & Dominic Amevor

House Dominic

A house the studio designed and visualized with and for two brothers, Adolph and Dominic Amevor, published on 2 June 2009 as a single dusk render while the building was still only a design. The studio called it “something we did with and for a good friend” and asked its readers to see the design before construction, which is why the archive holds one render and no completion.

The post is two sentences long and was filed as “SOMETHING FOR A GOOD FRIEND” on 2 June 2009, revised eight minutes later, with no labels. It reads, exactly: “Almost forgot to share this project. This is something we did with and for a good friend of ours Adolph Amevor and his brother Dominic Amevor sometime in 2008.” and “Construction should start soon and we are excited with the design we want you to see it before it’s built.” Nothing else about the commission was ever published.

The name is the studio’s, not the post’s. The blog never titles this project — “something for a good friend” is all it is called there — and it is listed here as House Dominic, after Dominic Amevor, the brother the work was done with and for. Adolph Amevor is named first in the post as the studio’s friend, so both brothers are carried as clients rather than one.

Three dates are in play and all three are kept. The prose says the work was done “sometime in 2008”; the render itself is stamped 2009 under its signature band; the post is 2 June 2009. The listing is filed to 2009, the year the studio printed on the drawing and the year it published, and the 2008 line is recorded here as the studio’s own recollection of when the work happened rather than quietly dropped.

The single image is a front view at dusk, and it is framed like a plate rather than a photograph: a black band across the top carries the.SEED.v1.0 over a hairline rule with 2009 beneath it at the left, and revivalgh.blogspot.com, the gold rounded mark and the REVIVAL wordmark at the right. Below it the house is white and cuboid with flat roof lines, its front broken by two piers of rough honey-coloured stone, dark brown banding, a slatted brown garage door, a brown pergola over the entrance where two yellow chairs sit on a paved step, railed first-floor windows and a balcony to the right. Interlock paving fills the foreground, a red-orange hatchback is parked at the right, and branches frame both sides.

That signature band ties this to the studio’s own identity work of the same months. The seed mark appears as the.SEED.v1.0 wallpaper of 1 October 2009, as THE SEED.1.1 across the GOIL frames, watermarked as the.SEED.V.1.1 / 2010 on the Takoradi renders, and printed as the SEED.V.1.1 in the footer of the studio’s own homepage. This is the earliest of the five to carry the v1.0 mark on a client commission.

The surname is already in this archive, and the two are not merged. Adolph’s Wedding Invite is a 2018 commission for Adolph Kwabla Amevor and Theresah Korkor Boatey at Odumase Krobo. This post gives no middle name, no city and no family connection, so the invite is listed as a possible sighting of the same friend rather than as the same client record.

Nothing after this post returns to the house. The feed does hold a post of 9 May 2009 called “SOMETHING FOR A FRIEND” — one month earlier and one word shorter — but its entire text is “Just a simple flyer we did for a friend”, which is a printed graphic and not this building. No designer is credited here, so there is no Team row; the post carries no labels, so there is no Software row either. The plate is the house’s own upload, a 1276 by 528 PNG, re-encoded for the web.

The source post is on blogspot — SOMETHING FOR A GOOD FRIEND, 2 June 2009.
