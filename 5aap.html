Architecture / Design + Build — 2020 · Studio Commission

5AAP

Rigorous spatial articulation balancing proportional harmony, light simulation, and bespoke detailing created for 5AAP.

5AAP forms part of RDVS Studios' enduring catalogue of architectural, interior, and computational spatial explorations. Completed in 2020, the project investigates tactile material boundaries and volumetric precision.

Our multidisciplinary team developed the project from programmatic schematic design through high-fidelity visual simulation and comprehensive turnkey project execution, ensuring strict adherence to the studio's closed-loop methodology.
