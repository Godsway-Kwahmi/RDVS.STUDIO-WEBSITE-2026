RDVSchool

The educational wing of RDVS Studios, training emerging architects, spatial designers, and 3D visual artists in industry-grade digital craft.

We believe exceptional architectural design requires uncompromising visual articulation. RDVSchool translates our commercial studio pipeline into rigorous masterclasses.

Taught directly by practicing architects and senior VFX supervisors, our curriculum eschews generic software tutorials in favor of deep optical physics, real-world construction tolerances, and emotive spatial storytelling.

Students work directly with actual architectural CAD packages, high-end shading libraries, and real-time visualization engines to build professional portfolios capable of competing on the international stage.

Core Masterclass Tracks

01. Photoreal 3D Architecture & Lighting

Ray-traced lighting models, PBR physical shaders, composition, daylight studies, and camera calibration.

02. Real-Time Spatial Environments (Unreal Engine 5)

Dynamic Lumen illumination, Nanite mesh optimization, interactive walkthroughs, and cinematic film output.

03. Interior Detailing & Millwork Modeling

Translating spatial interior concepts into buildable shop drawings, custom cabinetry joinery, and fabrication plans.
