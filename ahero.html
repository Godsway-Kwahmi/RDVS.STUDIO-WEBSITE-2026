Ahero

Distinctive sculptural mirror collection uniting expressive artisanal frames with refined reflective geometry.

The Ahero Mirror collection was designed and made by FK Living / AFK. Characterized by undulating timber contours and tactile handcrafted finishes, it establishes a bold dialogue between organic forms and modern interiors.

3D visuals, cinematic framing, and animation by RDVS Studios, highlighting the sculptural presence and specular highlights of the crafted silhouettes.
